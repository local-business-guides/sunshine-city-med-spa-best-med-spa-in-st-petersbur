What a visit may cover

A visit may include a discussion of your concerns, relevant options, and what a potential treatment involves. What is covered depends on your goals and the service being considered. Appointment length varies, so ask the office how much time to plan for when booking.

The cost depends on the service, the scope of care, and whether more than one visit is part of the plan. There isn’t one cost that applies to all concerns. Ask for information specific to the option being discussed before deciding how to proceed.

To compare medical spa providers, ask who will discuss your care, what experience they have with your concern, what outcomes are realistic, and what risks or follow-up you should understand. You can also ask what alternatives may be available. A thoughtful answer should make the process clearer, not pressure you into choosing a service.
