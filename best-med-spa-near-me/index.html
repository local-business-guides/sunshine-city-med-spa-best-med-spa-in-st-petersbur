Key facts to know before booking

  • What it covers: Medical spa care may involve discussion of facial rejuvenation, wellness, or pigmentation concerns. The appropriate options depend on your needs and the provider’s assessment.
  • How it works: Expect to describe your goals and concerns, discuss relevant background, and collaborate on a plan. Ask what each proposed service is intended to do and what follow-up may involve.
  • Timing: The time needed varies by service and by the plan you choose. Ask the office how much time to set aside for your visit and whether any later appointments may be recommended.
  • Cost factors: Costs generally depend on the service, the scope of the plan, and whether multiple visits are involved. Request details for the options being discussed rather than relying on a general estimate.
  • Terms you may hear: “Non-surgical facial rejuvenation” refers to approaches aimed at facial appearance without surgery. “Hyperpigmentation” means areas of skin that appear darker, while “melasma” is a type of pigmentation concern.

When comparing providers, look for clear explanations, a chance to discuss your priorities, and a plan that addresses your individual concerns. Ask how the provider approaches natural-looking results, what the limits of a service are, and what questions you should consider before deciding.
